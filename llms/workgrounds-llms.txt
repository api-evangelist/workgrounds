## About

Workgrounds uses AI agents to automate the most complex parts of group hotel bookings: sourcing hotels, comparing bids, negotiating group rates, and managing contracts. The platform is free for customers - hotels pay a placement fee.

Founded by Nikhil Sethi. Workgrounds serves enterprise teams including executive assistants, travel managers, finance teams, and employee experience leads who need to book group hotel accommodations for offsites, conferences, summits, and team events.

## Core Capabilities

- AI-powered hotel sourcing and bid comparison for group room blocks
- Automated RFP generation and hotel negotiation
- Self-serve room claims for guests (RSVP and claim rooms)
- Personal nights management (guests book and pay for personal extensions)
- Consolidated billing and reconciliation (one folio per room block)
- Contract parsing and normalization (resort fees, taxes, true total cost)
- Global hotel inventory access (any 3+ star hotel worldwide)
- HRIS integrations for capacity prediction
- Retail rate comparison and alternative date analysis

## Key Statistics

- 20-30% average savings on group hotel rates vs retail pricing
- Optional 15-minute conversation with our team
- 24-hour hotel bid turnaround
- 95%+ room block fill rates
- Customers include Ramp, SeatGeek, Lithic, CivicActions, Yieldmo, RevenueCat, Calm, Sidecar Health

## Pricing Model

Workgrounds is 100% free for companies booking group hotel rooms. Hotels pay a placement fee to access qualified group travel leads. No annual contracts, no hidden fees, no per-user charges.

## FAQ

Q: How does billing work?
A: Flexible. Workgrounds supports Centralized Billing (one master invoice for room & tax) or Individual Pay (guests pay their own way). You choose per trip.

Q: What counts as a room block?
A: 20 room nights is the minimum. That's when hotels start treating you like a group with contracts, attrition policies, and negotiated rates.

Q: If hotels pay you, how do I know you're showing me the best options?
A: Transparency is core to the model. Every bid is ranked by total value to your company, not by commission. You see the full comparison matrix and make the final call.

Q: Can hotels stay on email and send PDFs/Excel?
A: Absolutely. Hotels reply via email with attachments, and AI agents parse the data into your dashboard. No portal login required.

Q: How do you normalize taxes/fees?
A: AI agents read the fine print in PDF contracts to extract resort fees, urban fees, and taxes, giving you a true apples-to-apples total cost comparison.
